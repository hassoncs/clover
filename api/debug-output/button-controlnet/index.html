Testing silhouette preservation with different sizes and control strengths

How It Works

ControlNet Canny uses edge detection to preserve your silhouette shape:

1. Canny Edge Detection: Finds edges in your silhouette image
2. ControlNet: Forces Flux to follow those edges during generation
3. Control Strength: How strictly to follow the edges (0.0 = ignore, 1.0 = strict)

Optimal silhouette: Black shape on white background with sharp edges. Avoid internal colors/gradients - they confuse the edge detector.

What to Look For

✅ Good: Output matches silhouette shape exactly
❌ Bad: Shape is distorted or rounded
✅ Good: Sharp corners preserved
❌ Bad: Corners became rounded
✅ Good: Button size matches silhouette
❌ Bad: Much larger or smaller
✅ Good: Style applied (color, texture)
❌ Bad: Looks just like silhouette
