# Church Starter Kit: Amen — Bible Party Games

Welcome to **Amen**, the ultimate Bible party game platform designed to bring your congregation together through scripture, fellowship, and fun. Whether you're hosting a youth group night, a family devotion, or a church-wide social, Amen provides a safe, engaging, and educational environment for all ages.

## What is Amen?

Amen is a collection of 8+ interactive party games that can be played on smartphones, tablets, or computers. It’s designed for groups of 2 to 12+ players, making it perfect for small groups or large gatherings.

### The Games
- **The Great Hall of Wisdom** (Trivia): Fast-paced Bible knowledge challenges.
- **The Fellowship Table** (Quip): Creative and humorous fill-in-the-blank prompts.
- **Scrolls of Truth** (Fibbage): Discover surprising and obscure Bible facts.
- **The Book of Ages** (History): Guess the dates of major biblical and church history events.
- **Illustrated Scripture** (Drawing): Draw and guess scenes from the Word.
- **Who Am I?** (Heads Up): Guess the Bible character or event on your "forehead."
- **The Crossroads** (Dilemma): Discuss and vote on "Would You Rather" biblical scenarios.
- **The Council** (Ranking): Rank biblical topics and see how your group compares.

## Step-by-Step Setup

### 1. Create Your Organization Account
To get started as a church leader:
1. Visit [amen.games](https://amen.games) and click on **Church Plans**.
2. Select the plan that fits your congregation size.
3. Create an account using your church email address.
4. Once registered, you will have access to the **Church Dashboard** where you can manage your subscription and view usage stats.

### 2. Hosting Your First Game Night
Hosting is easy and requires no special hardware other than a screen to show the "Lobby" (like a TV or projector) and players' own devices.

1. **Launch the Game**: Log in to your account on a computer connected to your main screen.
2. **Select a Game**: Choose one of the 8 games from the library.
3. **Share the Join Code**: A unique 4-letter room code will appear on the screen.
4. **Players Join**: Instruct everyone to go to `amen.games` on their phones and enter the room code and their name.
5. **Start the Fun**: Once everyone is in the lobby, click **Start Game**!

## Tips for a Great Experience

### Recommended Group Sizes
- **Small Groups (2-6 players)**: Great for *The Crossroads* or *The Council* where discussion is key.
- **Youth Groups (6-12 players)**: *The Great Hall of Wisdom* and *Illustrated Scripture* are high-energy favorites.
- **Large Gatherings (12+ players)**: Use "Audience Mode" where extra players can join to vote and influence the game even if they aren't the primary players.

### Hosting Tips
- **Screen Mirroring**: Use an Apple TV, Chromecast, or a simple HDMI cable to put the game on the big screen.
- **Audio**: Turn up the volume! The music and sound effects add to the atmosphere.
- **Prizes**: Consider small prizes for the winners to keep the competition friendly and exciting.

## FAQ

**Is the content safe for all ages?**
Yes! All content is reviewed for theological accuracy and is designed to be ecumenical and family-friendly. We avoid "Red Zone" or controversial topics to ensure a welcoming environment for all Christian traditions.

**What are the technical requirements?**
Any device with a modern web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge) will work. We recommend a stable Wi-Fi connection for the best experience.

**Can we play remotely?**
Absolutely! You can host a game over Zoom or Microsoft Teams by sharing your screen. Players can join from their own homes using their phones.
