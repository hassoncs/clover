# Press & Influencer Kit: Amen — Bible Party Games

Welcome to the official press kit for **Amen**. We’re on a mission to bring scripture, fellowship, and fun to every youth group, family, and church gathering.

## Product Overview
Amen is a collection of 8+ interactive party games designed for groups of 2-12+ players. Built for the modern church, Amen turns smartphones into game controllers, allowing groups to engage with the Bible through trivia, drawing, guessing games, and deep discussions.

**Tagline:** Scripture. Fellowship. Fun.
**Website:** [amen.games](https://amen.games)
**Contact:** press@amen.games

---

## Key Features
- **8+ Unique Games:** From fast-paced trivia (*The Great Hall of Wisdom*) to creative drawing (*Illustrated Scripture*).
- **No Special Hardware:** Players join via their own mobile browsers at `amen.games`.
- **Theologically Sound:** All content is reviewed for accuracy and designed to be ecumenical and family-friendly.
- **Church-Scale Play:** Supports large groups with "Audience Mode" for voting and interaction.
- **Cross-Platform:** Works on iOS, Android, and Web.

## Pricing & Tiers

## Brand Guidelines

### Visual Identity
- **Primary Colors:** Deep Purple (#4C1D95), Gold (#FBBF24), White (#FFFFFF).
- **Tone:** Joyful, reverent but fun, community-focused, and modern.
- **Do's:** Focus on the "togetherness" aspect. Show people laughing and engaging with the Word.
- **Don'ts:** Avoid "cheesy" or dated church aesthetics. Don't use overly complex theological jargon in marketing.

---

## Screenshot & Video Guide
When capturing content for Amen, focus on these key screens:
1. **The Lobby:** Show the 4-letter join code and a list of players joining on their phones.
2. **The Great Hall of Wisdom:** A high-energy trivia question with the timer ticking down.
3. **Illustrated Scripture:** A funny or impressive drawing of a Bible scene (e.g., Noah's Ark or the Parting of the Red Sea).
4. **The Crossroads:** A "Would You Rather" prompt that sparks discussion.
5. **The Winner's Circle:** The final leaderboard showing the "Disciple of the Day."
