# Slopcade Landing Page Feature Brief

Working draft for design/marketing refresh focused on two audiences:
- Players and creators who want instant fun and creative control
- Investors who want to see technical moat, monetization leverage, and scalable infrastructure

## Brand Positioning (Put this above the fold)

### Core promise
- AI-powered game engine where anyone can create, remix, and play infinite games.
- Not just a game. A creation platform with real scripting, physics, and multiplayer infrastructure.

### Suggested hero copy directions
- "Make games. Break games. Play games."
- "From idea to playable game in minutes."
- "Fork anything. Reskin everything. Ship your own version."

### Hero proof bullets (fast scan)
- Prompt-to-play game generation
- Remixable game logic plus swappable visual themes
- Real-time party multiplayer
- Cross-platform: web, iOS, Android

## Feature Inventory for the Page

Use these as section blocks, cards, comparison tables, or animated walkthrough modules.

### 1) Prompt-to-Game Creation
- Users describe a game in natural language.
- Platform generates game logic, assets, and sounds.
- Built for fast creation loops: idea -> playable prototype -> iteration.

Why users care:
- Zero engine setup.
- Fast creative payoff.

Why investors care:
- AI-assisted creation lowers content production cost.
- Shorter time-to-content means faster catalog expansion.

### 2) Deep Remixability (Core Product Loop)
- Every game can be forked and modified.
- Scripts, physics, prefabs, and visuals are independent layers.
- Themes and asset packs can be swapped and reused across games.

Why users care:
- They can start from something fun and make it their own.
- Creative depth without requiring a full game-dev stack.

Why investors care:
- Strong UGC flywheel: fork -> remix -> publish -> discover.
- Network effects strengthen as the remix graph grows.

### 3) Real Scripting Power (Not a Toy)
- JS scripting model attached to prefabs (`scriptRef`).
- Physics-based gameplay architecture.
- Composable game definition model designed for iteration.

Why users care:
- Serious creators are not boxed in by templates.

Why investors care:
- Platform can serve both casual creators and technical tinkerers.
- Better retention potential than novelty-only generators.

### 4) Multiplayer Party Experiences
- Built-in multiplayer sessions and party-oriented gameplay flow.
- Host-and-play experiences designed for fast social fun.
- Supports the "play now, no heavy setup" entry point.

Why users care:
- Immediate social utility with friends.

Why investors care:
- Multiplayer creates stronger retention loops and session frequency.

### 5) Cross-Platform Distribution
- Single platform presence across web, iOS, and Android.
- Shared engine/infrastructure foundations across products.

Why users care:
- Play and create from anywhere.

Why investors care:
- Broader acquisition surface and reduced platform concentration risk.

### 6) Creator Economy and Monetization Rails
- Free and Pro membership tiers.
- Sparks credit system for AI generation usage.
- Revenue-sharing model for creators.

Why users care:
- Clear path from play -> create -> earn.

Why investors care:
- Multiple monetization levers: subscription, credits, and marketplace-style economics.

### 7) Technical Foundation as Moat
- Godot + React Native + QuickJS architecture.
- Durable Objects for real-time multiplayer state.
- Cloudflare Workers, D1, and R2 foundation.
- Supabase auth and Stripe/RevenueCat billing stack.

Why users care:
- Reliable experience across devices.

Why investors care:
- Strong execution credibility and extensible platform architecture.

## Investor-Facing Highlights (Dedicated section)

### Platform, not single-title risk
- Slopcade is a game creation platform with multiple product surfaces, not one bet on one game.

### Content scalability
- AI-assisted generation plus remix behavior can expand playable catalog without linear content headcount growth.

### Multi-layer monetization
- Subscription + credits + creator split model enables diversified revenue architecture.

### Defensibility vectors
- Tooling depth (editor + scripting + engine)
- User-generated remix graph
- Real-time multiplayer infrastructure
- Cross-platform runtime and distribution

## Suggested Landing Page Structure

1. Hero: clear promise + 3-4 proof bullets
2. "How It Works" strip: Prompt -> Generate -> Remix -> Play with friends
3. Feature blocks: creation, remixability, scripting depth, multiplayer, cross-platform
4. Investor proof section: monetization + platform leverage + technical moat
5. Creator economy section: pricing, credits, revenue share
6. Social proof/testimonials (once available)
7. CTA stack: "Start Creating" and "Play Featured Games"

## Claims Discipline for Launch Copy

Use this as a copy QA checklist before publishing:
- Keep hard metrics out unless validated by analytics.
- Present roadmap items as roadmap, not shipped capability.
- Lead with demonstrated strengths: creation speed, remixability, multiplayer, cross-platform, monetization rails.
- Keep tone energetic and slightly irreverent, while still investor-legible.
