APPROACH TAKEN: Read-alias fallback (forward-safe)
--------------------------------------------------
Added buildContentAudioR2KeyCandidates() to api/src/party/content/audio/r2-keys.ts.
For contentType='estimation', candidates returned are:
  1. audio/voice/{brand}/content/estimation/{id}.mp3  (canonical, tried first)
  2. audio/voice/{brand}/content/wager/{id}.mp3       (legacy fallback)
  3. audio/voice/{brand}/content/history/{id}.mp3     (legacy fallback)

Applied in party-content.ts linkAudioAssets route: if canonical key is missing
in R2, legacy keys are tried before marking the item as notInR2.

This ensures production environments where audio was generated under legacy keys
(wager/history) before T8 backfill will continue to have those assets accessible.
