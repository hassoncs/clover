The final report follows this structure:
1. Executive Summary: High-level overview of the "split-brain" typography system.
2. Support Matrix: Feature-by-platform comparison (Godot vs RN vs Web).
3. Current State Analysis: Detailed breakdown of Godot (Strong) vs RN (Weak).
4. Gap Analysis: Categorization of 14 gaps (Impl-gap vs Platform-limit).
5. Edge Cases & Risks: Analysis of race conditions, offline play, and CORS.
6. Verdict: Direct answer to the user's question with confidence level.
7. Recommended Additions: Prioritized roadmap with effort/risk assessment.
8. Caveats & Platform Notes: Technical and legal considerations.
