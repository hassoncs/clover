## Notes

The legacy `entityEffects` path applied raw GLSL shaders directly to entities via `bridge.applySpriteEffect()`. This approach:
1. Bypassed the canonical sprite effect dispatcher
2. Had no state management or lifecycle tracking
3. Used custom GLSL instead of predefined effect types

The canonical path uses:
- `EntityPrefab.effects` and `GameEntity.effects` for declarative effects
- `EffectStateGroup` for conditional effects
- Predefined `SpriteEffectType` vocabulary (glow, outline, tint, etc.)
- Proper lifecycle management via EntityFactory and GameBridgeEffects

The migrated games retain their `shaders` (hot-swappable shader library) and `graph` (post-process effects) which are separate systems from per-entity sprite effects.
